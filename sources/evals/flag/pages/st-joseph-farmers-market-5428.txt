We'll run from 3 to 6 pm on Fridays through October 16. No Market on Friday, July 3, as Americans will be celebrating the 250th anniversity of the July 4, 1776, Declaration of Independence. This season, we'll have 30+ producers and artisans. You can find us at the Lake Wobegon Trailhead in the Resurrection Lutheran Church parking lot on County Road 2 in St. Joseph. We are o ne of the oldest farmers’ markets in central Minnesota. Our Farmers Market will celebrate Dairy Day on Friday, June 12, and National Farmers Market Week on Friday, August 7, with special activities, live music, and more. Each Friday, we will have entertainment — music, kids’ crafts, sometimes magic. We hand out coupons ($2 each) to children and senior citizens, made possible by donations from our small business community. These coupons increase access to local foods, foster a fun environment to eat healthy, and financially support our local producers. Thank you, St. Joseph! We are proud to participate in the Farmers’ Market Nutrition Program, providing fresh vegetables, fruits and herbs to Women, Infants & Children (WIC) participants and low-income seniors. We offer Market Bucks, which match SNAP/EBT dollar-for-dollar up to $10.
