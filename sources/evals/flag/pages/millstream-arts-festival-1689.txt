Millstream Arts Festival is an outdoor juried art show held on the last Sunday in August in downtown St. Joseph, Minnesota. It is a festive environment with a wide variety of visual arts, literature and poetry, music, dance, enticing foods, hands-on art activities for children, and much more! Millstream is a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting visual and performing arts. Located in St. Joseph, Minnesota. Located in a little town north of the Twin Cities and west of St. Cloud, Millstream Arts Festival is worth the trip! Calling all makers, creators and artists!
