# DEBUG launch arguments

Extracted from CLAUDE.md 2026-09-04. A Release build reads none.
Read this file when you need a specific flag; CLAUDE.md points here.

> **Pruned 2026-09-17.** The strip-down deleted the Today feed modules, the Activities and
> Calendar features, and the map tab, and took their flags with them. Every flag below was
> checked against the source on that date. The dead ones are called out where their absence
> would otherwise read as a bug — a flag that stages a deleted view is worse than no flag,
> because it fails silently. `docs/archive/GUTTING-LEDGER.md` records what went and how to get it back.

**DEBUG-only launch arguments** (for headless verification). The app reads them in one place,
`BlockParty/Support/LaunchFlags.swift`, and a Release build reads none. `LaunchFlagsTests` fails when
a flag the app reads isn't here, or one here is read by nothing (move it to the Dead list). When
`-town-samples` and `-town-offline` are both passed, offline wins on every screen.

- **Shell and previews:** `-header-collapsed` pins Today's bar to its scrolled-away state — the wordmark, the friends mark, the map disc and the bell all gone, the band left to the feed and its soft edge gradient. There is no scroll automation here, so without it that state cannot be screenshotted at all; pair with `-feed-scrolled` for content under the band. (The flag was briefly deleted on 2026-09-21, while the bar was locked in place and had no scrolled-away state, and came back with the scroll-direction behaviour the same day.) `-open-tab town|daily|search|you` — **`-open-tab map` is dead**, the map is no longer a tab; `-tab-cycle` steps forward through the bar every 1.4s, wrapping, so the tab motion can be recorded headlessly (there is no tap automation here); `-open-map` raises the map cover on launch and is now the only headless way into the map, since the real entry is a tap on Today's top-bar map button; `-show-home`; `-show-skeletons`; `-share-demo`. Loading is a skeleton, and `-show-skeletons` is the flag that shows it. `search` opens the Search tab (its places load signed out too, with the public key). With it, `-search-words <text>` opens with those words typed (the results), `-search-focus` opens on the recents as after a tap on the field, and `-search-chip <all|people|businesses|events|groups|parks>` opens on that filter (`businesses` is the logo wall). Events there are DEBUG samples; groups are the Town's real ones (`get_groups`, read signed out too), and a group's card or result opens its Group page, whose Join group follows the same flags as Join below (under `-town-samples` it writes nowhere); typed words find real neighbours, or the sample ones under `-town-samples`; recents start from the mockup's five until something is searched. `daily` opens the Daily page as the App Store build shows it: today's real Event of the day (`get_event_of_the_day`, from the morning job's standouts; daily-tab 14) and no Spotlight, Suggestions or Following, which have no read yet, or the placeholder when there's nothing today. `-daily-samples` shows the sample Spotlight, Event of the day, Suggestions and Following instead, for design work. With it, `-daily-hour <h>` holds Daily's Town clock at that hour of today: the low sun (6 throws the Spotlight's shadow right, 21 left, midday straight down) and Event of the day's samples (8–12, 16–17 and 19–21; at 21 or later the section is gone) the Agenda's (7–10, 9–11, 14–15:30 and 19:30–21) Suggestions' (seven from 7:30 to 20:30), the same way, and Following's (events at 9–13, 15–16 and 17:30–18:30; updates posted at 7:15 and 11, shown once that hour has come), and `-tilt <x>,<y>` holds the phone's lean there (-1 to 1 each), since the simulator has no motion sensor: the Spotlight card leans and Search's logo shine sits where that tilt puts it (`PhoneTilt`). The Town feed lives on **`town`**, inside `FeedView`'s scroll under `TodayTopBar`. It reads `get_town_feed` (the Town's approved events, found ones included, in the database's order) in DEBUG and release alike, so with none to show it shows the empty state; `-town-samples` reads `DailyFixtures.townEntries` instead (the sample events, no postings, never the live database; views and opens go nowhere), for chrome and card work that needs photos under the bar. The Farmers Market sample (`e2`) carries a photo saved of its place, so the credit chip and Report photo show on fixed data. `-open-page town|city` pushes the Town's own page or its City's, as the Town bar's wordmark menu does (follows 04); with `-town-samples` both read samples. `-open-event <id>` pushes that Event's page once the Town feed has it (`e1` to `e7` under `-town-samples`, or a live Event's id), and `-open-business <place id>` (with `-open-tab search`) opens that business's page once Search has read it (its logo, read from the network, can take about 20 s to land in the top right), so a place-photos check needs no scrolling. `-town-offline` shows the same samples as the list kept from a last visit, with every read failing, so the bar's offline pill can be photographed (Edit profile's Save fails the same way, sending nothing, so its "Couldn't save your profile" shows); like `-town-samples`, it makes no account and Your code shows the sample owner. `-town-cancelled` (with `-town-samples`) calls off the first sample card (the red Canceled pill and black-and-white photo on it and its Event page), the photo-less City Council card (the red pill in its Event page's bar) and Rocktoberfest, three days on, in the You samples, so the You tab leaves it out and the app opens on the called-off alert until its OK is tapped (an eyes run reinstalls, which forgets the OK) (watch-every-place ticket 12). `-you-free-today` (with `-town-samples`) moves today's two You samples two days on, so today is empty and the You tab's Agenda shows the next plan under its line, "In 2 days" (you-tab 11). `-town-group` (with `-town-samples`) makes the first sample card the St. Joe Rod and Gun Club's, the real group's id, so its small Join beside the name and the club's Group page in Search are one entry (rsvp-and-groups 07); with `-town-refuse` that Join is refused too. `-town-games` (with `-town-samples`) puts four game cards first (game-card 02): SJU vs Gustavus to come, and the finals SJU 33–23 Concordia, Sartell 6–0 Sauk Rapids-Rice and Sartell's 2–2 tie with Anoka, the schools' saved logos read from the network. `-town-live` (with `-town-samples -town-games`) puts SJU vs Gustavus on today and replays its score and scoring plays every 4 seconds, through the same path a realtime update takes, to its final (game-card 05 and 07). `-open-team <uuid>` pushes that local school's page (school-activities 02). `-town-news` (with `-town-samples`) adds five of the social watch's news posts as the feed places them (town-feed 35): The Wandering Cow's after the second card, as one that shares your interests, and the rest after the last event, the places' logos read from the network (Stearns County has none: its first letter). `-town-menu-open` (with `-town-samples`) opens the first sample card's ••• sheet a second after launch, with its Score box, for a screenshot on the phone, which nothing here can tap. Join and the heart follow the same flags (`RealLifeActions.backend`): under `-town-samples` they succeed and write nowhere, under `-town-offline` every Join, heart and save gets no answer, so it stays tapped and keeps trying, with no shake (town-feed ticket 24; the shake is now only the server's "no", proven by the module's tests); `-town-refuse` (with `-town-samples`) has the server say no to every Join, RSVP and Join group, so the hold, the turn back and the shake can be recorded (rsvp-and-groups 01 and 06); both offer Join and the heart with no account.
- **Onboarding:** `-onboarding` shows the onboarding flow as a first launch would, while it is switched off (`RootView.showsOnboarding`, until BP app onboarding ticket 24); its saved step and finished flag live in `UserDefaults` (`onboarding.step`, `onboarding.finished`), so `-onboarding.step 0 -onboarding.finished NO` starts it on the Welcome on a test runner that keeps the app's data. `-onboarding-not-yet` holds the Next bar grey, so its shake can be recorded.
- **Today:** `-feed-scrolled` starts the feed 420pt down (add `-town-samples`: the real feed may be too short to scroll that far), which is the only way here to photograph the top glass fade — the soft scroll-edge effect only shows once content is actually underneath the bar; `-feed-scrolled-y <pt>` picks where that jump lands (and jumps on its own, without `-feed-scrolled`), so a state inside the 44pt fade band can be held still. `-feed-scroll-sweep` animates one 4-second scroll to 200pt and `-scroll-log` prints every offset/progress sample the scroll reports. **Run that pair after any change to the top bar's geometry:** a healthy sweep is ~241 samples with 0 direction reversals climbing 0 → 200. It is what caught the bar-height/inset feedback loop on 2026-09-19 (1420 reversals in 1422 samples, feed frozen at 1pt). `-feed-render-log` prints every body re-evaluation of the feed layers (`Self._printChanges`); `-frame-gap-log` counts dropped display frames via CADisplayLink — the sim substitute for Instruments' Animation Hitches, which refuses the simulator platform. `-feed-bar-back` holds the bar out over a `-feed-scrolled` feed, so the floating bar's edge can be shot still. The static card states are available for regression work via `-feed-card-gallery` and `-feed-card-autoplay`.
- **The social feed (on Town):** `-daily-feed-preview [-daily-feed-state mixed|postings|events|empty]` mounts `DailyView` over `DailyFixtures` with no auth and no network. The state argument is not a convenience: nothing here can drive a scroll, so without it only what comes first is ever reachable for a screenshot — `postings` brings a 4:5 posting card to the top, `events` the short overlay card, `empty` the onboarding state. This flag mounts the column full-screen with no top bar; to photograph it where it actually ships, use `-open-tab town`. The order is the fixtures' own: postings, then the events still on (the finished sample event is absent). Fixture content is INVENTED; only the town is real.
- **The map:** `-map-center <lat>,<lon>`, `-map-zoom <z>`, `-map-bearing <deg>` and `-map-pitch <deg>` start the camera elsewhere, closer or further, turned or tilted (the compass shows once it's turned); `-map-open-bubble <n>` opens the card of the nth of today's bubbles (0 the soonest) once they've loaded. The map is a full-screen cover with its own close X, and `-open-map` is what raises it (BP app references/map-reset).
- **The event page:** `-event-page-two-photos` gives every event page a second, bundled photo (`centennial-park`) in place of the Google venue photo, and skips that lookup, so the photo carousel exists with no network. It is for `EventCardPhotoTapTests`, whose edge swipe must start on a carousel; Town's cards look the same with or without it.
- **Signed-out data:** `-anon-data` — with NO session, `AuthStore.validAccessToken()` hands back the shipped public anon key so a signed-out simulator can still photograph data-bearing states. Places, the Town feed's events and the map's events no longer need it: their reads fall back to the public key on their own, as release does. Reads go exactly as far as RLS lets the anon role (`places`/`club_events` allow anon SELECT); any write is refused server-side like any anonymous caller. DEBUG-only, compiles out in release.
- **Map clock:** `-map-now HH:MM` pins the map's clock there at launch and lets it tick on, so a live bubble and a countdown can be recorded at any hour.
- **Logos:** add `-poi-logo-stub` for deterministic offline logos (`POILogoCache`).
- **Profile:** `-open-profile` with `-profile-settings` (opens Settings), `-profile-bottom` (Settings, scrolled to Sign out), `-profile-edit`, `-profile-edit-interests`, `-profile-moderation` or `-profile-autoclose`. `-review-queue-preview` shows the admin Review queue over the City's real found events with no account or network (its only real entry is an admin's profile); add `-review-queue-fail` to make every decision fail, so the row coming back with a shake can be recorded.
- **Hello pill** (hello-drop): any launch flag keeps the day's hello away, so other runs' frames stay as they were; `-hello-drop` shows it on every launch and `-hello-welcome` shows the first open's welcome. `-hello-drop-hold` keeps it 10 s, so a recorded tap lands on the message. `-hello-seen-days N` acts as if the last open was N days ago. `-hello-later` is a later open, the day's hello already said: 8 s after launch a sample final and a sample contact who joined are found. `-hello-final win|loss` drops Saint John's final against Gustavus 8 s after launch, and `-hello-moment <name>` (`firstEvent`, `tenthEvent`, `firstFriend`...) drops that moment. A forced pill checks contacts who joined only with `-hello-joined`, since each check spends one of the Contacts page's 5 a day.
- **Test runs:** `-ui-tests` marks a UI test run: the app makes no account and sends no tap kept from before (`AuthStore.isTestRun`), and the Town bar runs without its blur, whose paused animation would hold every XCUITest step to its 60 s idle timeout.
- **Today bar's friends / notifications:** `-open-friends` pushes the Friends page (2026-10-13,
  BP app docs/plans/friends), and `-friends-words <text>` opens it with those words typed. It
  finds neighbours on the live database for a real account; `-town-samples` gives it sample
  neighbours on town photos instead (two asking you, three friends, one you asked, and a
  BP-yellow dot on the friends mark), and `-town-offline` makes every search and friends read
  fail. With `-town-samples`, `-friends-fail` makes every Add friend, Cancel, Confirm and
  Delete fail (the hold, flip back and shake) and `-friends-limit` makes Add friend hit the
  daily limit (Apple's alert) (2026-10-13, friends 03).
  `-open-friends-chat` pushes the sample inbox and then its newest chat (2026-10-06), the one
  way left into the sample chats. `-open-notifications` raises the screen behind the bell, and
  `-notice-samples` fills it (and the hello pill's memory) with the sample changes. Their only
  real trigger is a tap, so without these there is no way to screenshot them and no way to add
  them to `DynamicTypeAuditTests`' walk — which is exactly the reason their ledger entries say
  `notYetAudited`. Pair either with `-open-tab town`.
- **Appearance** (System/Light/Dark; its switch went with the town menu on 2026-10-25, so these flags are the only way to set it): `-appearance system|light|dark` makes the persisted write — relaunch WITHOUT the flag to prove it stuck; `-appearance-demo` walks the three states on a timer so the live, no-relaunch repaint can be recorded.
- **Data mutation:** `-seed-places` runs the DEBUG place seeder. Treat it as a backend write, not a harmless screenshot flag.
