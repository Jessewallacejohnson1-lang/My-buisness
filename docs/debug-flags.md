# DEBUG launch arguments

Extracted from CLAUDE.md 2026-09-04. All compile out in Release.
Read this file when you need a specific flag; CLAUDE.md points here.

> **Pruned 2026-09-17.** The strip-down deleted the Today feed modules, the Activities and
> Calendar features, and the map tab, and took their flags with them. Every flag below was
> checked against the source on that date. The dead ones are called out where their absence
> would otherwise read as a bug — a flag that stages a deleted view is worse than no flag,
> because it fails silently. `docs/archive/GUTTING-LEDGER.md` records what went and how to get it back.

**DEBUG-only launch arguments** (for headless verification; all compile out in Release):

- **Shell and previews:** `-header-collapsed` pins Today's bar to its scrolled-away state — the wordmark, the friends mark, the map disc and the bell all gone, the band left to the feed and its soft edge gradient. There is no scroll automation here, so without it that state cannot be screenshotted at all; pair with `-feed-scrolled` for content under the band. (The flag was briefly deleted on 2026-09-21, while the bar was locked in place and had no scrolled-away state, and came back with the scroll-direction behaviour the same day.) `-open-tab town|daily|search|you` — **`-open-tab map` is dead**, the map is no longer a tab; `-tab-cycle` steps forward through the bar every 1.4s, wrapping, so the tab motion can be recorded headlessly (there is no tap automation here); `-open-map` raises the map cover on launch and is now the only headless way into the map, since the real entry is a tap on Today's top-bar map button; `-show-home`; `-show-skeletons`; `-show-map-intro`; `-share-demo`. `-open-speeddial` and `-speeddial-loop` were deleted on 2026-09-24 with the compose speed dial, when posting was paused. `-show-loader` and `-show-loading-cover` were deleted on 2026-09-21 with the launch loader and the full-screen tab loading cover themselves; loading is a skeleton now, and `-show-skeletons` is the flag that shows it. The launch splash and its `-launch-splash-hold` / `-launch-splash-skip` flags were deleted on 2026-09-18: `LaunchScreen.storyboard` is bare paper now, so there is nothing to hold or skip. `search` opens the Search tab (its places load signed out too, with the public key). With it, `-search-words <text>` opens with those words typed (the results), `-search-focus` opens on the recents as after a tap on the field, and `-search-chip <all|people|businesses|events|groups|parks>` opens on that filter (`businesses` is the logo wall). Events there are DEBUG samples; groups are the Town's real ones (`get_groups`, read signed out too), and a group's card or result opens its Group page, whose Join group follows the same flags as Join below (under `-town-samples` it writes nowhere); typed words find real neighbours, or the sample ones under `-town-samples`; recents start from the mockup's five until something is searched. `daily` opens the Daily page as the App Store build shows it: today's real Event of the day (`get_event_of_the_day`, from the morning job's standouts; daily-tab 14) and no Spotlight, Suggestions or Following, which have no read yet, or the placeholder when there's nothing today. `-daily-samples` shows the sample Spotlight, Event of the day, Suggestions and Following instead, for design work. With it, `-daily-hour <h>` holds Daily's Town clock at that hour of today: the low sun (6 throws the Spotlight's shadow right, 21 left, midday straight down) and Event of the day's samples (8–12, 16–17 and 19–21; at 21 or later the section is gone) the Agenda's (7–10, 9–11, 14–15:30 and 19:30–21) Suggestions' (seven from 7:30 to 20:30), the same way, and Following's (events at 9–13, 15–16 and 17:30–18:30; updates posted at 7:15 and 11, shown once that hour has come), and `-tilt <x>,<y>` holds the phone's lean there (-1 to 1 each), since the simulator has no motion sensor: the Spotlight card leans and Search's logo shine sits where that tilt puts it (`PhoneTilt`). The Town feed lives on **`town`**, inside `FeedView`'s scroll under `TodayTopBar`. It reads `get_town_feed` (the Town's approved events, found ones included, in the database's order) in DEBUG and release alike, so with none to show it shows the empty state; `-town-samples` reads `DailyFixtures.townEntries` instead (the sample events, no postings, never the live database; views and opens go nowhere), for chrome and card work that needs photos under the bar. The Farmers Market sample (`e2`) carries a photo saved of its place, so the credit chip and Report photo show on fixed data. `-open-event <id>` pushes that Event's page once the Town feed has it (`e1` to `e7` under `-town-samples`, or a live Event's id), and `-open-business <place id>` (with `-open-tab search`) opens that business's page once Search has read it (its logo, read from the network, can take about 20 s to land in the top right), so a place-photos check needs no scrolling. `-town-offline` shows the same samples as the list kept from a last visit, with every read failing, so the bar's offline pill can be photographed. `-town-cancelled` (with `-town-samples`) calls off the first sample card (the red Canceled pill and black-and-white photo on it and its Event page), the photo-less City Council card (the red pill in its Event page's bar) and Rocktoberfest, three days on, in the You samples, so the You tab leaves it out and the app opens on the called-off alert until its OK is tapped (an eyes run reinstalls, which forgets the OK) (watch-every-place ticket 12). `-town-group` (with `-town-samples`) makes the first sample card the St. Joe Rod and Gun Club's, the real group's id, so its small Join beside the name and the club's Group page in Search are one entry (rsvp-and-groups 07); with `-town-refuse` that Join is refused too. `-town-games` (with `-town-samples`) puts four game cards first (game-card 02): SJU vs Gustavus to come, and the finals SJU 33–23 Concordia, Sartell 6–0 Sauk Rapids-Rice and Sartell's 2–2 tie with Anoka, the schools' saved logos read from the network. `-town-menu-open` (with `-town-samples`) opens the first sample card's ••• sheet a second after launch, with its Score box, for a screenshot on the phone, which nothing here can tap. Join and the heart follow the same flags (`RealLifeActions.backend`): under `-town-samples` they succeed and write nowhere, under `-town-offline` every Join, heart and save gets no answer, so it stays tapped and keeps trying, with no shake (town-feed ticket 24; the shake is now only the server's "no", proven by the module's tests); `-town-refuse` (with `-town-samples`) has the server say no to every Join, RSVP and Join group, so the hold, the turn back and the shake can be recorded (rsvp-and-groups 01 and 06); both offer Join and the heart with no account.
- **Today:** `-briefing-preview [-briefing-state sample|one_event|zero_events|voted|none|degraded]` mounts the real Today composition from a canned payload with no auth/network. **The payload still loads and the states still resolve, but the registry is empty**, so what you photograph is `TodayTopBar` over an empty feed — the flag now verifies the chrome and the read contract, not module layout. `-feed-scrolled` starts the feed 420pt down (add `-town-samples`: the real feed may be too short to scroll that far), which is the only way here to photograph the top glass fade — the soft scroll-edge effect only shows once content is actually underneath the bar; `-feed-scrolled-y <pt>` picks where that jump lands (and jumps on its own, without `-feed-scrolled`), so a state inside the 44pt fade band can be held still. `-feed-scroll-sweep` animates one 4-second scroll to 200pt and `-scroll-log` prints every offset/progress sample the scroll reports. **Run that pair after any change to the top bar's geometry:** a healthy sweep is ~241 samples with 0 direction reversals climbing 0 → 200. It is what caught the bar-height/inset feedback loop on 2026-09-19 (1420 reversals in 1422 samples, feed frozen at 1pt). **`-header-hairline` is deleted** (2026-09-19) along with the bar's hairline itself: the scroll edge effect is what separates the bar from the feed now, and the rule read as a seam where the fade ended. `-feed-render-log` prints every body re-evaluation of the feed layers (`Self._printChanges`); `-frame-gap-log` counts dropped display frames via CADisplayLink — the sim substitute for Instruments' Animation Hitches, which refuses the simulator platform. Two more still compile but have nothing to act on with an empty registry: `-feed-focus <moduleID>`, which hid the modules above the one being inspected, and the `-trivia-sample|-trivia-answered|-trivia-streak|-trivia-low-sample` fixtures, which `TriviaModel` still parses although its card is deleted. **Dead with their views:** `-briefing-gallery`, `-gallery-state`, `-feed-gallery`, `-feed-state`, `-townnotes-gallery`, `-townnotes-gallery-page`, `-foryou-sample`, `-foryou-no-tags`, `-foryou-empty`, and the almanac family (`-almanac-write|-almanac-static|-almanac-fail`, `-almanac-part`, `-almanac-demo-line`). The legacy card feed remains available for regression work via `-feed-card-gallery`, `-feed-card-autoplay`, and `-today-feed-preview|-today-feed-empty|-today-feed-skeleton`.
- **The Day Schedule sheet:** the Your Day section is deleted from Today, and the Horizon card it carried, kept parked until then, was deleted on 2026-10-19 with its galleries, demos and mock lane (`docs/archive/GUTTING-LEDGER.md` says how to get it back). The sheet is unchanged and is now reachable **only** by flag, since the rail that opened it is gone: `-day-sheet-preview [-day-sheet-state upcoming|inprogress|completed|empty]` (the `cta` state went on 2026-09-24 with the sheet's "Add to today" button); `-day-sheet-hold` opens it once and stays; `-day-sheet-demo` loops open → scroll → tick → dismiss. Pair sheet flags with `-show-home` for the real tab shell. **Note:** `record_sim_video` fails in this setup (it starts, then can't report the output path) — use `xcrun simctl io <udid> recordVideo` instead.
- **The social feed (on Town):** `-daily-feed-preview [-daily-feed-state mixed|postings|events|empty]` mounts `DailyView` over `DailyFixtures` with no auth and no network. The state argument is not a convenience: nothing here can drive a scroll, so without it only what comes first is ever reachable for a screenshot — `postings` brings a 4:5 posting card to the top, `events` the short overlay card, `empty` the onboarding state. This flag mounts the column full-screen with no top bar; to photograph it where it actually ships, use `-open-tab town`. The order is the fixtures' own: postings, then the events still on (the finished sample event is absent). Fixture content is INVENTED; only the town is real.
- **Utility row (parked):** `-utility-row-preview` renders the row full-screen, bypassing the auth gate; `-utility-expand|-utility-customize|-utility-empty` stage its states. The subsystem moved to `Features/Civic/Parked/Utility/` and is unreferenced on purpose, waiting on the Civic tab — these flags are how you confirm it still works before wiring it back in. See `Features/Civic/Parked/README.md`.
- **Map browsing and camera:** `-map-sheet places`; `-map-detent peek|medium|full`; `-map-open <spotid>` (`downtown|saintbens|chapel|wobegon|millstream|saintjohns`); `-map-open-poi [name-substring|id]`; `-map-detail-expanded` (with either open flag — starts straight in the full-details presentation the "View full details" row opens); `-map-filter all|food|parks|events|saved` starts the map on a chip; `-map-center <lat>,<lon>`; `-map-zoom <z>`; `-map-bearing <deg>`; `-map-pitch <deg>`; `-map-autozoom`. These now land inside a full-screen cover rather than a tab — the map carries its own close "X" leading in the top chrome, and `-open-map` is what raises it.
- **Map search:** `-map-search-open` expands the search field on appear (keyboard up); `-map-search <q>` also types the query so the results panel renders; `-map-search-committed <q>` programmatically commits the first result — fly + pin open (retries briefly while POIs load).
- **The event page:** `-event-page-two-photos` gives every event page a second, bundled photo (`centennial-park`) in place of the Google venue photo, and skips that lookup, so the photo carousel exists with no network. It is for `EventCardPhotoTapTests`, whose edge swipe must start on a carousel; Town's cards look the same with or without it.
- **Signed-out data:** `-anon-data` — with NO session, `AuthStore.validAccessToken()` hands back the shipped public anon key so a signed-out simulator can still photograph data-bearing states. Places, the Town feed's events and the map's events no longer need it: their reads fall back to the public key on their own, as release does. Reads go exactly as far as RLS lets the anon role (`places`/`club_events` allow anon SELECT); any write is refused server-side like any anonymous caller. DEBUG-only, compiles out in release.
- **Map marker/rain states:** repeatable `-map-save <spotid>` and `-map-force-live <spotid>`; `-town-rain` drives the real map interaction; `-town-rain-preview` bypasses auth and loops the physics; add `-poi-logo-stub` for deterministic offline logos. See `docs/town-rain-reference-measurements.md`.
- **Town menu/profile:** `-open-menu` with optional `-menu-autoclose` — the only entry into the menu since Today's avatar was removed on 2026-09-18, which also killed `-tap-menu` and `-slow-tap`; `-open-profile` with `-profile-expand|-profile-bottom|-profile-edit|-profile-edit-interests|-profile-moderation|-profile-autoclose`.
- **Today bar's friends / notifications:** `-open-friends` pushes the Friends page (2026-10-13,
  BP app docs/plans/friends), and `-friends-words <text>` opens it with those words typed. It
  finds neighbours on the live database for a real account; `-town-samples` gives it sample
  neighbours on town photos instead (two asking you, three friends, one you asked, and a
  BP-yellow dot on the friends mark), and `-town-offline` makes every search and friends read
  fail. With `-town-samples`, `-friends-fail` makes every Add friend, Cancel, Confirm and
  Delete fail (the hold, flip back and shake) and `-friends-limit` makes Add friend hit the
  daily limit (Apple's alert) (2026-10-13, friends 03).
  `-open-friends-chat` pushes the sample inbox and then its newest chat (2026-10-06), the one
  way left into the sample chats. `-open-notifications` raises the reserved screen behind the bell
  (added 2026-09-20 as `-open-search`; search became a tab on 2026-09-29). Their only
  real trigger is a tap, so without these there is no way to screenshot them and no way to add
  them to `DynamicTypeAuditTests`' walk — which is exactly the reason their ledger entries say
  `notYetAudited`. Pair either with `-open-tab town`.
- **Appearance switch** (the town menu's System/Light/Dark control, which has no tap automation): `-appearance system|light|dark` makes the same persisted write a tap makes — relaunch WITHOUT the flag to prove it stuck; `-appearance-demo` (pair with `-open-menu`) walks the three states on a timer so the live, no-relaunch repaint can be recorded.
- **Onboarding:** none left. The signed-in wizard, the 20-screen pre-auth flow, the component bench and the motion bench were deleted on 2026-09-18, and every `-show-onboarding` / `-onboarding-*` / `-bp-*` flag went with them.
- **Data mutation:** `-seed-places` runs the DEBUG place seeder. Treat it as a backend write, not a harmless screenshot flag.
